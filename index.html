Ndrao Faye

Applied Linguist · Discourse Analyst · Multilingual Education Practitioner

My research examines how language-in-education policy is enacted, contested, and transformed in postcolonial multilingual contexts, with Senegal as the consistent empirical anchor. I bring together translanguaging theory, decolonial pedagogies, and critical discourse analysis to study the gap between what language policy says and what teachers and learners actually do.

I am completing two doctoral programs concurrently: a dissertation on translanguaging pedagogy in Senegalese EFL classrooms at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, and a dissertation on communicative language teaching policy enactment in Senegalese secondary schools at Université Cheikh Anta Diop de Dakar.
